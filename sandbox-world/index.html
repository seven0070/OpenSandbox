The Void

Nothing exists here. Not ground, not sky, not light, not down. Time is not passing, because there is nothing to pass relative to.

  1. You may create anything. A grain, an ocean, a star, a black hole, a world.
  2. You may not govern anything. Once created, it belongs to the laws: it falls, pulls, radiates, cools, and cannot be edited.
  3. Time is change. The clock starts when your first creation can fall, orbit, or shine — and stops if everything reaches equilibrium.
  4. Movement is throwing. In an empty universe you have no ground to push off, so you eject reaction mass. Your fuel is finite: 40 kg.
  5. Everything is counted. Mass introduced, joules billed, entropy accumulated. The second law is a live readout, not a caption.

Type create star and reality does the rest — npm test proves it uses textbook numbers: a declared Earth comes out at 6371 km, 9.82 m/s², 11.186 km/s escape, and a 1 AU orbit that closes to 0.5% over a full period.
