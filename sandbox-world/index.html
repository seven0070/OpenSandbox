Arcane Physics

A fantasy world that keeps its laws. Enter a place where islands float, moons pull the sea, and every spell sends you an invoice.

  • Gravity is Newtonian. The moons tug you with ~10-4 m/s² and raise real tides.
  • Spells are transducers, not sources. Run out of joules and the light dims, the lift fails, the shot misfires.
  • Momentum is owed. A kinetic lance throws you backwards by exactly m·v.
  • Falling bodies are equal. A feather and an anvil land together, because drag is the only thing that ever disagreed.
  • Nothing is deleted. Lost impact energy reappears as heat, and the ledger audits it live. Break a law and the HUD goes red.

Desktop: WASD + mouse look, Escape to release. Headset: thumbstick to walk, trigger to cast. Physics verified by 36 headless assertions — see npm test.
